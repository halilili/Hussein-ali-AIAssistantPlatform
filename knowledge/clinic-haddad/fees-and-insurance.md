# Fees and Insurance

## Consultation fees
The consultation fee depends on the doctor and is confirmed by the booking system for each doctor.
A follow-up visit for the same problem within 14 days of a consultation is charged 50% of the consultation fee.

## Test prices
- Resting ECG done during the visit: 20 USD
- Echocardiogram (heart ultrasound): 90 USD
- Exercise stress test: 120 USD
- 24-hour Holter monitor: 100 USD

## Payment methods
We accept cash in USD, cash in LBP at the daily rate posted at reception, and Visa or Mastercard cards.
We do not accept cheques.

## Insurance
We work directly (no upfront payment) with CedarCare Insurance, Phoenicia Assurance and the MedNet TPA network.
NSSF (CNSS) patients are accepted with a valid prior approval; please bring the approval form and your NSSF card.
For other insurance companies, you pay at the clinic and we give you a detailed invoice and a medical report
for reimbursement.
