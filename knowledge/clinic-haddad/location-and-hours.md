# Location and Opening Hours

## Address
Hamra, Bliss Street, Cedar Building, 3rd floor, Beirut. The building is opposite the pharmacy on the corner
and has an elevator. The clinic is wheelchair accessible.

## Parking
A valet service is available in front of the building from 09:00 to 18:00 for 3 USD.
The nearest public parking is 150 meters away on Makdessi Street.

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday: 09:00 to 18:00. Saturday: 09:00 to 13:00. Sunday and official holidays: closed.
Each doctor has their own schedule inside these hours; the booking system shows the real free times.

## Contacting the clinic
WhatsApp is answered by the virtual secretary 24/7. To talk to a person, call the clinic phone during opening hours.
WhatsApp is not monitored for emergencies.
